About Gemini

Built by Google DeepMind, Gemini is a family of natively multimodal AI models designed to understand and reason across text, code, audio, images, and video.

Early 2023 — Origins as Bard & Google DeepMind

Google's conversational AI journey began in March 2023 with the launch of Bard, initially powered by LaMDA and PaLM 2. In April 2023, Google merged its two leading AI research labs—Google Brain and DeepMind—to form Google DeepMind, unifying efforts to build next-generation artificial intelligence.

December 2023 — The Launch of Gemini 1.0

In December 2023, Google introduced Gemini 1.0 in three sizes—Ultra, Pro, and Nano. Unlike earlier systems trained separately on different data types, Gemini was built from the ground up to be natively multimodal.

February 2024 — Bard Becomes Gemini & Gemini 1.5

Google officially rebranded Bard to Gemini in February 2024 to reflect the underlying model technology, alongside the release of Gemini 1.5 Pro, which introduced a breakthrough long-context window capable of processing massive documents, codebases, and videos in a single prompt.

2024–2025 — Agentic AI with Gemini 2.0 & 2.5

With Gemini 2.0 (December 2024) and the Gemini 2.5 series (2025), Google expanded into real-time multimodal voice and vision interactions (Gemini Live), Deep Research capabilities, and advanced reasoning ("Deep Think") for complex coding and scientific tasks.

2025–2026 — Gemini 3 and Gemini 4 Generations

The Gemini 3 series (launched in late 2025, followed by 3.1 updates in early 2026) pushed reasoning and software engineering benchmarks to new highs, paving the way for the Gemini 4 generation in late 2026 with deeper integration across Google Search, Workspace, and Android.
